Digits 0-9 of the Hershey "Script 1-stroke" vector font (A. V. Hershey, U.S. National Bureau of Standards; free to use), taken from the
JSON port in the npm package "hersheytext" by James T. / techninja (MIT licence, https://github.com/techninja/hersheytextjs).
build/digits.json holds only those ten glyphs as lists of pen strokes; build/extract.js shows how it was made.
