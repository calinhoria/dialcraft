# Forged Steel

Amazfit Bip 6 watchface, 390 × 450. Edit `project/Forged Steel.wfproj.json` in Dialcraft and keep future revisions in this sample folder. The Zeus source export is in `out/forged-steel/`. The original dial and numeral artwork are in `sheets/`.

Version 1.0.1 draws the heart-rate and battery numbers with image widgets driven by the watch sensors. Heart rate shows two dashes until a measurement is available. Zeus compiled the export, and a simulated watch runtime verified live updates and missing heart-rate data. Physical-watch verification is pending.
