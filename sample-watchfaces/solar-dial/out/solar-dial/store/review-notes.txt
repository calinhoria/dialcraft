Solar Dial is a watch face made with Dialcraft, a watch face designer for Zepp OS, and built with the Zeus CLI for Amazfit Bip 6 (390x450, rounded-rectangle screen).

- The preview pictures show the face in its normal (non-AOD) state at 10:09:36, in the shape of the screen, with transparent corners.
- Data it shows, all read from the watch's own sensors: Sunrise, Sunset.
- Contains animation, drawn by the face itself; it stops in always-on mode.
- It has its own always-on display (AOD) design.
- The face itself makes no network requests, has no app-side or settings page and stores nothing about the user. Weather and sun times are the values the watch already receives from the Zepp app.
